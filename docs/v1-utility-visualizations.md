# Utility visualizations

This reference owns implemented utility selection/projection, current display intent, line/bar/area and compatible stack editing, presets, and inventory capture. See [foundation](v1-visualization-foundation.md) for shared meaning, [persistence](v1-visualization-persistence.md) for transfers/commands, [workbench](v1-visualization-workbench.md) for lifecycle, and the [phase plan](v1-visualizations-and-home-plan.md) for remaining breadth.

## Selection and authoritative values

Global facility inclusion bounds resource catalogs, data resolution, grid-rate demand, and latest-data anchors. Empty inclusion never means All. Meter sources store an explicit selection method; a cleared Source selection is absent/setup-required rather than an unrestricted rule. Fixed meters retain exact GUIDs. Fixed groups retain group identity and follow current membership; matching rules explicitly include future matches. Missing anchors require repair rather than broader scope. Retired historical meters are included by default. New matching series use stable defaults while resource-specific overrides remain effective.

Latest-data anchors use provider-validated observations, including real zero, excluding unavailable/post-retirement observations. Portfolio fiscal periods follow account settings and facility periods follow facility settings. Units and energy treatment declare follow-owner or pinned values. Monthly/yearly data uses canonical calendarization/conversions rather than a new formula boundary.

Emissions require authoritative inclusion evidence; display flags alone do not establish coverage. Saved-input checks inspect actual energy/volume fields. V1 calculation requests combine the loaded bundled grid catalog with current custom rates and include those rates in freshness. Grid-dependent previews await catalog readiness and reject missing coverage rather than accepting fallback zero.

Native observations retain bill identity and actual dates without invented prorating. Native charges retain individual meter/charge amount or supported usage/demand meaning; monthly charge allocation, cross-meter category totals, and summed demand are unavailable.

Partial totals show valid isolated contributions and omitted-input reasons. No valid observations means unavailable, never zero. Ambiguous/invalid normalized inputs cannot justify computed observations. Preserve missing, excluded, retired, estimated/mixed, duplicate, partial, and alignment-padding meanings. Separate sources may compare overlapping contributions; totals deduplicate meters and block double counting. Scientific formulas and source records remain authoritative.

A monthly calendarized utility configuration can also render with one predictor configuration under the [predictor combination contract](v1-visualization-foundation.md#predictor-observations). That path shares whole-month bounds and the newest eligible anchor across both categories, retains valid empty contributions, and permits different original units on an unstacked axis with no unit label. Meter-only selection, dates, warnings, aggregation, and unit compatibility retain their existing behavior.

## Definition compatibility and display overrides

Current greenfield intent includes saved visibility and owner-qualified appearance overrides. Each selects exactly one resource GUID or stable provider-local series ID. Provider IDs identify generated series, including categories and known resource-bearing utility IDs; presentation selectors never become required source anchors. Resource overrides win over category overrides resolving to the same series. Explicit nonnegative integer order precedes new/unordered matches; appearance-only edits preserve provider ordering, and absent order never moves a member.

Definitions use a current greenfield schema without historical decoder/upgrade adapters. Database structure, record envelopes, and backup container versions remain distinct and unchanged by this display extension.

Overrides outside current matching membership stay dormant rather than becoming missing-source anchors. Copy/backup remaps known resource appearance references and qualifies unmapped ones to prevent accidental activation in another account. Ordinary local source/total category appearance follows the copied account. Known resource-bearing utility provider IDs retain their resource namespace and rebuild their GUID suffix through typed meter/group/facility maps. Unmapped IDs retain original owner qualification. The known utility Unassigned category embeds a facility GUID and is rebuilt only through the explicit facility map. Unmapped Unassigned identities preserve owner qualification, visibility/order/appearance, and dormancy without adding an anchor; previously foreign overrides remain foreign/dormant. Charge metric selectors use explicit mapped charge identity; unknown selectors and blocked references retain original intent. Unknown definitions remain preserved through persistence.

## Supported behavior and limitations

| Capability | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Calendarized | Energy, compatible consumption/water, cost, location/market emissions; monthly/yearly, owner fiscal settings, fixed/rolling/history, and supported comparisons. |
| Native bills | One meter's dated bill occurrences. Multiple measures from that meter share its occurrence alignment; different meters are not implicitly paired. Native comparisons/group totals are unavailable. |
| Named charges | Recorded amounts for supported categories; consumption/sewer quantity uses the bill editor's starting unit and electricity demand its demand unit. Fee/tax and generic usage/MDQ quantity without an established bill-editor unit, and summed demand, are unavailable. |
| Live series | Wildcards discover future matches. Resource/provider-qualified labels, colors, axes, visibility, and ordering persist; absent overrides stay dormant. |
| Capture | Unfiltered inventory Energy/Water/Cost charts preserve actual resource identities, grouping, selection, trace order, reproducible values, and representable appearance/settings. Search/status filters, unassigned groups, unsupported dash patterns, and changed quality interpretations explain and block capture. Both meter Monthly Chart views support the naming/save/workbench journey and conditional source links described below. Zoom remains temporary. |
| Editing | Invalid source/date drafts survive metadata saves; same-configuration external changes require explicit saved/local review. Recovery protects incomplete child fields. |

Energy usage trend and Utility cost trend prefill the same Add decisions and permit Source, Meter group and Individual meters paths with explicit resource binding. Source choices retain live matching rules; groups retain GUIDs and live membership; meters retain exact GUIDs. Owned resources without readings remain selectable. Portfolio cards identify named/unnamed facilities and retired meters. Opening completed decisions or reselecting equivalent choices retains bindings and names; actual dependency changes review affected resets first. Standard meter measures use calendarized data automatically; Add has no observation-view step. Add collects selected measures before naming. Chart-wide units and Site/Source settings continue in Chart appearance. Each resource checkbox applies immediately through serialized autosave; Include All selects the current catalog independently of local search and does not save a live all-resources rule. Explicit removal deletes item presentation so re-adding starts with defaults, while live membership changes retain dormant overrides.

Time-series definitions save `defaultTraceType`; declarations and current-owner item overrides may supply `traceType`, with precedence item override, declaration, then chart default. Configured summaries derive Line, Bar, Area or Combination plus stacking mode from selected items, including hidden/no-data items and excluding dormant/foreign overrides. Unstacked Line, Bar and Area traces can render together; axes containing bars/areas or stacks include zero. Chart-wide type actions clear current-owner type exceptions, including dormant exceptions, while preserving other styling. Interval-only changes preserve item types.

Meter Data To Chart offers Energy, Consumption / water, Cost and Bill charge; location/market emissions appear only while the account display preference enables them, without rewriting saved intent. Generated energy series labels use the resource name without an Energy suffix or site/source prefix; the configured measure and treatment remain authoritative. One Meters section shares resource selection and supports multiple measures. Its Data settings contains compatible facility aggregation and named-charge settings. Chart appearance owns shared owner-relative/pinned units per quantity and Site/Source energy treatment. There is no calendarization toggle. Selecting Bill charge uses bill dates automatically and selects charges separately from monthly measures. Charges keep one explicit meter without Include All; replacing that meter reviews clearing its named charge before selecting a charge from the new owner; switching back to regular measures restores calendarized data. Existing review protects customized traces and incompatible comparison/stacking settings.

Each resource/measure combination has a sibling named trace section that combines trace type, axis, visibility, label, color, line pattern and keyboard-accessible ordering. Calendarized utilities support compatible absolute/percentage stacks under the shared [time-series contract](v1-visualization-foundation.md#time-series-presentations); native bills/charges retain unstacked presentations. Unsupported combinations preserve intent with specific explanations. Calendarized Scatter/Correlation/Heatmap settings follow [exploration visualizations](v1-exploration-visualizations.md). Calendarized breakdown bars/donuts and Treemap/Sunburst are implemented under the [composition contract](v1-composition-visualizations.md); further capture locations and Home remain pending. Tooltips identify partial coverage, original values, periods, quality and derived shares. Data-check findings appear in the workbench header; printing is chart-focused. Zoom remains temporary and survives compatible appearance edits.

Purchased compressed air remains unavailable where the existing canonical formula yields nonfinite emissions. Correcting that formula is separate work. Existing v0 behavior, report outputs, weather retrieval, and Electron IPC are unaffected by this visualization boundary.

## Monthly meter capture

The main Monthly Chart offers Visualize, then one Save visualization slideout with an editable `{facility name} - Monthly {meter name}` default and Save and open confirmation. Opening/canceling does not persist. The adapter binds exact account/facility/meter GUIDs, calendarized monthly all-history intent, independent supported energy/consumption/water/cost configurations, pinned displayed units and site/source treatment, labels, theme colors, axes, line/bar types, smoothing, legend visibility and configured hidden items. Automatically suppressed cost is not introduced. Series controls and legend interactions both participate in capture; at least one series must remain visible. Source zoom does not become a saved date range.

The feature checks source-owned monthly coverage, values, units and quality against the saved utility provider. Specialized measures, distance, changed coverage, unreproducible allocation and changed quality interpretations explain and block capture; no specialized provider or formula is added. The fiscal-year comparison below the main chart is also supported as Monthly patterns; other chart locations remain outside these slices. A changed reviewed source requires reopening the slideout. Confirmed creation reuses one proposal through write retries and publication recovery. A failed handoff opens the already saved identity without recreating it. Intentional later captures are independent and names need not be unique.

Both meter captures persist their source descriptor and original chart baseline. View source chart appears after capture/reopen only when the normalized chart configuration matches that baseline, excluding the visualization name. Title, styling, visibility, units, sources, measures and dates participate; reverting an edit restores the link. New readings and owner fiscal settings do not change saved intent. The regular link opens the correct chart anchor with normal leave protection; no chart-control, zoom, scroll or focus restoration is retained. Missing ownership or unverifiable provenance omits the link, including older records without metadata. The naming slideout contains no repeated chart-settings summary.

### Monthly patterns by fiscal year

Monthly patterns is a distinct chart choice in Add and the workbench, and can be captured from the combined fiscal-year chart under the main Monthly Chart. It supports one selected meter, meter group or utility source with one Energy or Consumption/water measure and optional Cost. Group/source patterns combine the matching meters through the canonical utility aggregation and retain live membership. A single Standard or saved Weather predictor is also supported as one panel, retaining saved units, zeros, signed values, duplicate exclusions and gaps without weather retrieval. Utility and predictor categories are alternatives, not mixed panels. Both panels share year colors, labels, line patterns and visibility. Only lines are supported, with optional smoothing; capture uses the source chart’s straight lines. Costs suppressed by the source are not invented. Unsupported specialized measures and changed quality interpretations block capture.

This view uses all history and includes future years automatically, without a second comparison, stacking or an interval toggle. Exact meters, meter groups and predictors use their current owning facility’s month order and start/end fiscal-year labels even with portfolio inclusion. Utility-source patterns use the visualization owner’s calendar: facility for a facility scope, account for a portfolio scope. Units and Site/Source can be pinned independently of that live calendar. Twelve month slots preserve zeros, gaps and actual dates; provider observations retain their original quality. Whole missing years are not invented from alignment padding. Capture preserves known-year colors and hidden years; newly discovered years use the existing palette. The Years controls and interactive legend update the same linked visibility. Year overrides remain dormant when their data disappears, and replacing the selected resource or source category reviews clearing those overrides.


## Validation

Protect selection/overlap, periods/fiscal meaning, actual inputs, real zero versus unavailable/partial data, units/charges, overrides/dormancy, repeated transfers, invalid-field protection, and capture fidelity with focused deterministic tests. Native coverage protects IndexedDB copy/backup/reopen, active-preview Worker lifecycle, and composed source-data journeys. Check charts, keyboard/focus, themes, responsive layouts, and printing where changed. Select commands and broader gates through [testing policy](testing.md) and the validation planner; historical per-stack execution exceptions do not define future validation policy.
