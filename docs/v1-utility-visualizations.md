# Utility visualizations

Implementation specification for [Vis4 #2905](https://github.com/ORNL-AMO/VERIFI/issues/2905), with the first line/bar slice of Vis6 and inventory capture slice of Vis9. See the [phase plan](v1-visualizations-and-home-plan.md), [foundation](v1-visualization-foundation.md), and [workbench](v1-visualization-workbench.md).

## Accepted decisions

Fixed meter selections retain exact GUIDs. Fixed meter-group selections retain group identities and intentionally follow current membership. Matching rules explicitly include future matching resources; missing anchors require repair. Retired historical meters are included by default. New matching series appear with stable defaults; resource-specific display overrides remain effective.

Latest-data anchors use provider-validated observations across sources, including real zero, and exclude unavailable or post-retirement observations. Portfolio fiscal periods follow the account; facility periods follow the facility. Settings declare follow-owner or pinned values. Monthly and yearly values use canonical calendarization and conversions. Emissions require explicit inclusion evidence from the authoritative projection; display flags do not establish calculation coverage. Saved-input checks follow the source’s actual energy or volume inputs. The shared v1 request now combines the loaded bundled grid catalog with the current snapshot’s custom rates, as approved during implementation; rates participate in input freshness, while formulas and result shapes remain unchanged. Grid-dependent previews await initial catalog readiness and reject missing coverage rather than accepting fallback zero. Native observations retain bill identity and actual date meaning without invented prorating. Native detailed charges remain individual meter/charge amounts or applicable usage/demand; monthly allocation, cross-meter charge-category totals, and summed demand are unavailable.

Partial totals expose valid isolated contributions and omitted-input reasons. No valid observations means unavailable, never zero. Existing scientific formulas are unchanged: saved-input evidence must justify a computed observation; ambiguous or invalid normalized values remain unavailable. Missing, excluded, retired, estimated/mixed, duplicate, partial, and alignment-padding meanings remain visible. Overlapping sources may be compared, but a source total deduplicates meters and combined double-counted totals are blocked.

## Definition compatibility

Version 2 adds saved visibility and owner-qualified presentation overrides. Each override selects exactly one domain resource GUID or stable provider-local series ID. Provider IDs identify category series such as utility sources and unassigned groups; they are never domain GUIDs or required source anchors. Resource overrides take precedence over provider-ID overrides targeting the same resolved series, and explicit nonnegative integer order precedes unordered/new matches. Appearance-only edits preserve provider order; absent order never moves a member. Version 1 decodes into equivalent current intent in memory; opening alone does not write. A real edit saves the current version through the existing command boundary. Overrides outside the source's current matching set remain dormant and are not required source anchors. Copy/backup remaps known resource presentation references and qualifies retained unmapped overrides so they cannot activate in another account. Ordinary local source/total category appearances follow the copied definition's account. The known utility Unassigned category embeds a facility GUID: copy/backup rebuilds that identity only through the explicit facility map, preserving appearance, visibility and order. An unmapped Unassigned identity keeps its original owner qualification and stays dormant across account transfers without adding a required source anchor. Previously foreign appearances remain unchanged and dormant. Historical duplicate proposals compare decoded equivalent intent, leaving the original version-1 record unchanged. Native charge metric selectors follow explicitly remapped charge identities; unknown selectors and blocked source references retain their original intent. Unknown definitions remain preserved. Database structure, record envelopes, and backup container versions are unchanged. Older builds cannot edit version-2 definitions.

## Supported boundaries

| Capability | Current behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Calendarized series | Energy, compatible consumption including water, cost, location/market emissions; monthly/yearly, owner fiscal settings, fixed/rolling/history and supported comparisons. |
| Native series | One meter's dated bill occurrences. Several measures from that meter may share its occurrence alignment; different meters are not implicitly paired. Native comparisons and grouped totals are unavailable. |
| Named charges | Recorded amounts for all supported categories; consumption/sewer quantities in the bill editor's starting unit and electricity demand in its demand unit. Fee/tax, generic usage/MDQ quantities without an established bill-editor unit, and summed demand are unavailable. |
| Live series | Wildcard selections discover future matches. Resource- or provider-ID-qualified labels, colors, axes, visibility and order remain presentation intent; absent overrides stay dormant. |
| Capture | Unfiltered inventory Energy/Water/Cost charts with faithful saved resource identities, representable line styles and reproducible measured values. Search/status filters, unassigned group traces, unsupported dash patterns, and changed quality interpretations explain and block capture. |
| Editing | Invalid local source/date fields stay protected through metadata saves. External changes to that same configuration require an explicit saved/local choice. |

Pre-configured trends require explicit resource selection and create independent definitions. Energy usage trend first offers Source, Meter group or Meters as icon cards, then multi-select cards for eligible resources with saved energy history. Source selections save a matching utility-source rule and source grouping; meter groups save fixed group GUIDs with live membership and group grouping; individual meters save exact meter GUIDs and meter grouping. Utility cost trend keeps individual-meter selection, also presented as cards. Opening a previous step immediately clears later selections and the draft name, even when the same view or facility is chosen again. Opening the grouping step clears selected resources; opening resource selection keeps its current choices and clears the later name. Additional utility sources can be added; unsupported families may be explicitly removed without changing their source records. Chart zoom and temporary trace-panel state are not saved. Quality notices use an expandable list so dense portfolios retain a usable preview, with partial coverage called out visibly.

Underlying values use the shared pagination control and include all rows for printing. Explicit resource changes replace obsolete exact-series anchors with a live selection and retain same-grouping resource appearances as dormant overrides. Changing grouping resets resource-specific preferences because their identity namespace changes.

Purchased compressed air remains unavailable when the existing canonical formula produces nonfinite emissions. This slice does not repair or replace that formula. Recovery requires an explicit decision before discarding incomplete child fields, and inventory capture retains trace order as well as appearance.
